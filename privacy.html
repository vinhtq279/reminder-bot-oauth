Privacy Policy – Reminder Bot

Reminder Bot is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Google Calendar (events) and Google Tasks scopes to read, create and update the owner's own events and tasks.

Use and storage

Data is used only to show agendas and send reminders to the owner via Telegram. It is processed on the owner's own server, is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored only on that server.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
